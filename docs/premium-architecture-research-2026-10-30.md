# Premium architecture research and design application

Reviewed: 30 October 2026. Scope: official practice websites and selected project/editorial pages, with desktop visual inspection of John Pawson and Olson Kundig. This is a qualitative design review, not a measured accessibility, SEO or performance audit.

## John Pawson — proportion, light and restraint

Pawson's official biography describes an approach concerned with mass, volume, surfaces, proportion, geometry, light and the experience of everyday objects. The homepage's visual opening gives architecture almost the entire screen, with a small practice name and very little interface. The architecture index provides categories, search, project names and dates.

Design application: give each architectural image room; reduce competing labels; align headings, photographs and captions; treat whitespace as part of the composition. Use a measured type scale and thin, quiet rules rather than decorative frames.

Sources: [Official biography](https://www.johnpawson.com/office/john-pawson), [Homepage](https://www.johnpawson.com/), [Architecture index](https://www.johnpawson.com/architecture).

## Olson Kundig — people, place, experience and craft

The official homepage connects design with people, place and experience, then routes visitors toward work, practice, people and process. The design approach page discusses kinetic design, sustainability/building performance, and craft/collaboration. These are the firm's stated practices, not claims about K & K Associates.

Visual limitation: the homepage's media could not play in the review browser; its text and navigation remained visible. Therefore this review does not certify its film playback or animation quality.

Design application: introduce a clear narrative under the visual opening. A featured study should pair an immersive image with a brief, type, and a route into its story. Motion needs a role: directing attention, showing a transition, or communicating a choice. Keep reduced-motion behavior and a pause control.

Sources: [Homepage](https://olsonkundig.com/), [Design approach](https://olsonkundig.com/design-approach/).

## SJK Architects — context, material and climate

The Hotel in Bodh Gaya case study gives credits and project information, then explains cultural ideas, material choices, local craft, passive climate strategies and water management. The relevant editorial lesson is that a project page explains decisions, rather than presenting images alone. The page's engineering and performance statements were not independently verified in this review.

Design application: add an original design lens on light, material and landscape. Relate each theme to the illustrative image on screen and to a practical design question. Do not copy the firm's project text, imagery, awards or credentials.

Source: [Hotel in Bodh Gaya](https://www.sjkarchitects.com/hospitality-architecture/hotel-in-bodh-gaya).

## What changed for K & K

- A shared page width and gutter system, with deliberate desktop, tablet and phone spacing.
- More consistent header, footer, image, caption and enquiry alignment.
- A calmer warm-white/ink palette retaining the professional blue accent.
- The existing interactive zoom collection, refined typography and reduced interface clutter.
- A new featured architecture presentation: four selectable studies, large crossfading images, concise summaries and individual project links.
- A new interactive design lens: Light, Material and Landscape, with keyboard-operable tabs and corresponding imagery.
- Aligned photo-viewer cards, replacing competing staggered baselines.
- A larger, clearer two-column desktop portfolio and single-column phone portfolio.
- More consistent project-page typography, metadata and story width.

The design uses existing authorised local assets. AI concepts and editorial reference images remain labelled. No practice history, team credentials, awards, project results, dimensions or client relationships were invented. The reference practices are research influences only; no affiliation is implied.

## Content still needed for a fully credible public practice website

Actual commissioned project photography and drawings; verified project facts and roles; team biographies and credentials; approved service scope; office contact information; an agreed process narrative. Until supplied, this remains a clearly labelled concept presentation. Existing local enquiry storage remains a preview workflow; email delivery is not connected.
